Tables in your notes and a calmer, more readable calendar.

## ✨ New

- **Tables** – Markdown (GFM) tables show as a real grid in formatted mode: a bold header on a tinted row, columns as wide as their text and aligned as the `:--` / `:-:` / `--:` row says, long cells wrapped when the table is wider than the note. Bold, italic, code, links and tags work inside cells. Move the caret into a table (arrows or a click on a cell) and it turns back into its Markdown source to edit, with the `|` dimmed; move out and it is a grid again. The line numbers show the table's first and last line with dots between them. *Copy as HTML* gives a real table, *Copy as plain text* tab-separated rows that paste into a spreadsheet.
- **Full view on macOS** – **Ctrl+Cmd+F** switches full view on and off (F11 belongs to the system there).

## 🎨 Calendar

- The hatching of past days in the week strip now runs across the tag bands, making a finer woven pattern.
- Tag colours are softer in the week strip (the full calendar, tag rail, legend and history keep the full colours).
- Day numbers of past days in the week strip are readable again, also in the dark theme: a darker (light theme) or lighter (dark theme) shade of the day's tag. Past days without notes are grey, future days light grey.

## 🛠️ Fixes

- Linux/macOS: no empty space above tables and image previews in the editor.
- Linux/macOS: the line-wrap mark in the line numbers is grey like the numbers, not a blue emoji.
- Windows: the strikethrough button in the toolbar shows a proper S with a stroke (the stroke used to land beside the letter).
