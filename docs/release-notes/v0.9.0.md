Share from your phone straight into your notes, see the open tasks of the whole notebook in one list, a month view of its own – and the end of the fading texts on Windows.

## ✨ New

- **Share from Android** – share a link, a selection, a text or pictures from any app on the phone and it becomes a note tagged `Share` (`Share_HHmm.md`, pictures in the day's `assets`).
  - On the same network the phone sends it **straight to WolfMark on your computer**, encrypted (Settings → *Sharing from the phone*, off by default). Otherwise it goes through OneDrive; without a connection it waits on the phone and is sent once.
  - The computer **says when a share arrives**: a notice *Shared from the phone* with the title and an *Open* button, and on Windows a system notification when the window is hidden or not in front.
  - The Android app also **shows your notes, read-only** – by day, with the week band, the month, tag tabs and rendered Markdown, in the look of the desktop app (light and dark). It never changes or deletes a note.
  - How to build and set it up: [`android/README.md`](../../android/README.md).
- **Open tasks in one list** – *Open tasks* in the history shows the tasks themselves: each note that has some, followed by its tasks.
  - The box **ticks a task off** from the list; the task's text opens the note with the caret on its line.
  - The tag and day filters narrow the list, and the number of all open tasks is next to the switch.
  - **Day cells** of the week band and the month show the number of open tasks in the corner; screen readers say it.
- **The month is a view of its own** – the calendar button shows the month with the history under it and no note. A day picked there narrows the history; a note picked from the history (or opened by search, a link or `Ctrl+N`) brings the week view back on that note's week.

## 🔄 Changed

- **RAW / FORMATTED is one switch** – two joined segments, the mode that is on a solid block. Before, the two buttons differed by a shade and did not say which one was on.
- **A slimmer header** – the date on the left and the calendar's controls with the week or month title on the right share one row.
- **Today brings the app back to how it starts** – today, its week and month, no tag, day or open-tasks filter. It leaves week or month as it is.
- **The app always starts on the week**, whatever was open when it was closed.
- **Full view** hides the date row too; the margins are the same on both sides.
- **The search index is rebuilt once** on the first start (it now also holds the tasks). Your notes are not touched – the index is only a cache.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Windows: day numbers, button texts and fills were at times drawn faded** – at half their strength, differently after every click on a day. The hatching of past days and the dotted lines were drawn with a kind of brush that made WinUI fade other parts of the window; they are now drawn as plain lines and look the same.
- **Windows: a click on the editor mode that was already on switched both off.**
- **The week band was redrawn some forty times for one change**; it is redrawn once, and a day that did not change is left alone.
- **A day cell could stay lighter** after the pointer had left it while it was hidden.
- **Opening the month** showed the month loaded the last time for a moment.
- **Opening the month with the mouse** let the keyboard focus fall to the first tag tab; it goes to the selected day.

## 🛠️ Under the hood

- **A new project, `android/`** (Kotlin + Compose): everything that needs no Android lives in a plain JVM module with unit tests. The rules for note file names, tag colours and the share protocol exist twice – in C# and in Kotlin – and both are checked against the same test vectors in `testdata/`, so they cannot drift apart ([ADR 0017](../adr/0017-android-share-lan-onedrive.md)).
- **Sharing over the network** is AES-256-GCM over TCP with nothing but the base class library; the computer announces its address and key to the phone through a file in the notes folder – no QR code, no discovery protocol.
- **CI builds and tests the Android app**; the release attaches an APK when a signing key is configured.
- 83 new test cases (720 in total).

## ⚠️ Good to know

- The Android app needs **your own Microsoft app registration** to sign in to OneDrive; see its README.
- The cause of the fading on Windows is in WinUI (Windows App SDK 2.6.5), not in WolfMark; the app now avoids what triggers it.
- Not verified on a real machine yet: the new switch and the month view on **macOS**.
