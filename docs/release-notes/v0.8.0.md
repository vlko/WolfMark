Quick notes from anywhere, links between notes, find and replace, a trash you can restore from – and a thorough pass over everything that could lose a note.

## ✨ New

- **Quick note** – a small window for a thought in the middle of something else: it starts a new note of today, saves as you type and gets out of the way with `Esc`.
  - **Windows:** a global shortcut (`Win+Shift+N` by default, off until you switch it on in Settings → *General* → *Quick note*; press the keys you want in the box).
  - **macOS and Linux:** assign `wolfmark --quick-note` to a shortcut of the system (Settings shows the exact command).
  - *Continue in the main window* opens the note there in full view.
- **Links between notes**
  - `[[2026/11/16/work_1403]]` (what *Copy link* writes), `[[work_1403]]` – the newest note of that name – and `[[2026-11-16]]` for a day; `[[target|label]]` shows a label of its own.
  - `Ctrl`+click follows a link (or *Open linked note* in the right-click menu). A link to nowhere says so.
  - A note shows **which notes link to it**: *Linked from 3* under its time opens the list.
- **Find and replace in a note** – `Ctrl+F` and `Ctrl+H`: `Enter` or `F3` goes to the next match, with `Shift` to the previous one, `Esc` closes. The counter shows *3 of 12*; *Replace all* is one step to undo.
- **Trash with restore** – Settings → *Trash* lists the deleted notes with their title, day and tag, **restores** one to where it was (never over another note) and empties the trash. You can let WolfMark remove notes after 30, 90 or 365 days; by default it never does.
- **`wolfmark://` links and the jump list** – `wolfmark://today`, `day/2026-11-16`, `note/…`, `new` and `search?q=…` open WolfMark where they point (switch the protocol on in Settings → *General* → *Markdown files*). *New note*, *Today* and *Search* are in the taskbar jump list on Windows, the Dock menu on macOS and the launcher's menu on Linux.
- **Text width** – Settings → *Editor* → *Text width* keeps lines to about 60, 80, 100 or 120 characters and centres the text in a wide window.
- **New app icons** – 13 redrawn icons to choose from; the white **W** is the default. The choice is now also in the Linux app.
- **macOS and Linux catch up with Windows**
  - **Calendar by keyboard** – arrows move between days, `Enter` opens one; days are announced by screen readers.
  - **Start at sign-in** (Settings → *General*), hidden in the notification area if closing to it is on.
  - **Settings:** excluded projects, real-time watching and the interval for Claude Code; index status and diagnostics to copy for a bug report.
  - **Editor:** *Start writing…* in an empty note; copying a selection takes its Markdown along (`**bold**`, not just `bold`); `Ctrl`+click ticks a task in raw mode.
  - **History:** a chip shows what the list is filtered by and clears it.

## 🔄 Changed

- **Deleted notes go to WolfMark's own trash on every system** – a folder next to your notes (`.wolfmark/trash`) instead of the Windows Recycle Bin. That is what makes restoring from the app possible, and because the folder travels with your notes, a note deleted on one PC can be restored on another. Notes you deleted with earlier versions are still in the Windows Recycle Bin. What you *remove* from the trash goes on to the Recycle Bin on Windows ([ADR 0016](../adr/0016-one-trash.md)).
- **Moving the notes folder restarts the app** right after the move, as switching portable mode does – nothing may be written into the old folder any more. A move that fails says why and leaves the notes where they were.
- **The search index is rebuilt once** on the first start (it now also holds the links between notes). Your notes are not touched – the index is only a cache.
- **Shortcuts read the Mac way on a Mac** – `⌘K` instead of `Ctrl+K` in tooltips and hints.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **A note changed elsewhere while you were typing could be overwritten.** The question *Load from disk / Keep mine* appeared, but the next autosave wrote your version over the other one before you answered – and a change that arrived a moment before a save was not noticed at all. Saving now checks that the file is still what the editor last saw and waits for your answer. If you move on without answering, your text is saved as a note of its own next to the other one ([ADR 0014](../adr/0014-external-changes.md)).
- **On a network share or a removable drive a deleted note could be gone for good** – Windows has no Recycle Bin there. The new trash folder works everywhere.
- **Moving the notes folder or switching portable mode could lose a Claude Code note** that the import was writing at that very moment; the import is now stopped and waited for first. The copy is verified by content, not just by size.
- **A damaged `settings.json` silently switched WolfMark to the default notes folder**, and a damaged `tags.json` was replaced on the first colour change. Both are now kept as a copy (`settings.broken-<time>.json`) and the app tells you.
- **A start that could not open the index or read the tags left empty lists without a word.** The window now says what went wrong.
- **Renaming a tag** dropped the conflict mark of a OneDrive conflict copy, and a rename that failed part-way did not always give the notes back their exact names.
- **Linux:** renaming a tag only in its letter case next to a note that already had that name could leave a note under a name the app did not see.
- **The file watcher went silent after an error** – for example when a network share or OneDrive disconnected – until the app was restarted. It also missed notes moved into a hidden folder and two renames in a row.
- **macOS and Linux:** memory grew while scrolling the history; a closed window of a Markdown file from outside stayed in memory; with the month open in a low window the history was drawn over the status bar.
- **Excluding the project `C:\work` from the Claude Code import also excluded `C:\workshop`.**
- README said a search result could be inserted as a link into the note – that was never there; `Ctrl+K` with text selected makes the selection a Markdown link.

## 🛠️ Under the hood

- **A project-wide review** went through the storage, index, sync and both apps; what it found is milestone M11 in [`BACKLOG.md`](../../BACKLOG.md).
- **One code base for what both apps do the same way** – settings, quick note, moving the notes, the texts around a note, screenshots – now lives in `WolfMark.Presentation` with tests instead of twice in the two UI projects.
- **Releases are tested first**: the release workflow runs the unit tests and the smoke tests (Windows and Linux) on the very binaries it publishes, and adds `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
- **Smoke tests** gained: a note changed by another program, a start with damaged settings, an index that cannot be opened, and screenshots of the Linux app (it draws its windows into images, so it works without a screen).
- **Benchmarks** (`benchmarks/WolfMark.Benchmarks`): lexing a line takes about 70 ns, opening a 5 000-line note 0.6 ms.
- More than 160 new test cases (637 in total). Third-party licences are listed in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`, shipped with both apps.

## ⚠️ Good to know

- Not verified on a real machine yet: anything on **macOS** in this release (start at sign-in, `⌘` in the texts), a live screen reader on Linux, and the trash on a network or removable drive on Windows.
- Find and replace works in the main editor, not yet in the quick note or in windows of Markdown files from outside.
- Still Windows only: spell check and a global quick-note shortcut of the app's own.
