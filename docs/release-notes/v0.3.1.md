WolfMark on Windows has a new Markdown editor, written from scratch and drawn with Win2D/DirectWrite. It replaces the RichEdit editor: every line is laid out on its own and only the lines on screen are drawn, so long notes stay as fast as short ones.

## ✍️ New editor (Windows)

- **Instant typing, even in long notes.** On a 5,000-line note a keystroke typically shows in ~2.5 ms (RichEdit: ~20 ms, with spikes of 140 ms and more on every Enter). Opening such a note takes ~0.1 s, switching Raw ↔ Formatted is immediate.
- **Spell check** with the Windows spell checker – Slovak and English at once (a word is only marked when every language finds it wrong), now also in long notes. Right-click a marked word for suggestions, *Add to dictionary* or *Ignore*.
- **Images** – previews right in the note. A line holding only an image behaves like a single character: the caret stands before or after it, arrows step over it, text typed beside it goes to a new line. Paste screenshots or image files, drag & drop from Explorer, double-click a picture to open it.
- **Line numbers** and **marker chips** – on the caret line the hidden markers (`H1`, `**`, `]↗` …) show as small chips, nothing shifts.
- **Tasks** – click a `[ ]` box to tick it off (Ctrl+click in Raw mode).
- **Context menu** – copy as plain text or HTML, change the line type (paragraph, headings, quote, lists, task) and inline formats, the current ones checked. Copy and Cut give Markdown including the hidden markers.
- **Typing in any language** – dead keys, AltGr characters, input methods (IME) and the emoji panel (Win + .).
- The caret keeps its column on ↑/↓, the view stays in place when switching modes, and a note opens scrolled to its end.

## ⚡ Faster and smaller

- **Windows download about half the size** (zip 52.8 MB → ~29 MB): only the Windows App SDK parts WolfMark uses, WinUI texts only in English and Slovak.
- Native AOT tuned for speed, .NET 11 runtime-native async.
- No more short freeze a moment after typing stops: after an autosave the history list updates the changed note only.
- The window appears right away; the last note loads into it afterwards.

## 🛠️ Other

- **Settings → Diagnostics** shows the version and environment, with a button to copy them for a bug report.
- Linux/macOS: notes and settings no longer land in the current folder when `~/.local/share` or `~/Documents` does not exist yet.
- `--trace-editor` writes editor timings to `logs/editor-trace.txt`.

## Known limitations

- The new editor does not support screen readers (UI Automation) yet.
- The Linux and macOS apps keep their AvaloniaEdit editor; they get the history and data-folder fixes above.
